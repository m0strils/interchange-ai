# Late Checkout Policy

> Self-authored demo corpus for the A2A demonstration; not any real hotel's policy.

## Standard checkout time
Standard checkout at Demo Hotel is 11:00 am. Guests staying past checkout
without an approved late checkout are subject to the fees described below.

## Requesting a late checkout
Guests may request a late checkout at the front desk or through the mobile app
any time after check-in. Late checkout until 2:00 pm is available at no charge,
subject to housekeeping and next-guest availability, and is not guaranteed
until confirmed by the front desk. Requests are approved on a first-come basis
starting the morning of departure; requesting the night before does not
reserve a slot.

## Fees past 2:00 pm
A checkout between 2:01 pm and 6:00 pm is billed a half-day room charge (50%
of the nightly rate). A checkout after 6:00 pm is billed a full additional
night at the rate on file for the stay. These fees apply whether or not a late
checkout was requested in advance.

## Loyalty tier exception
Members of Demo Hotel's top loyalty tier (Platinum) receive a guaranteed 2:00
pm checkout with no availability check, confirmed automatically at check-in.
This benefit does not extend to checkout times past 2:00 pm; requests beyond
2:00 pm still depend on availability and are billed per the fee schedule
above.

## Same-day sellouts
On nights the hotel is fully booked for the following arrival, late checkout
requests past 11:00 am may be declined entirely to allow time to prepare the
room for the incoming guest, including for Platinum members. The front desk
will note this at check-in when occupancy is expected to be full.

## Luggage storage as an alternative
Guests who cannot get a late checkout may store luggage at the front desk at
no charge and continue using hotel common areas until actual departure,
without incurring a late checkout fee.
