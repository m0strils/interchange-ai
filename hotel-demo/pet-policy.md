# Pet Policy

> Self-authored demo corpus for the A2A demonstration; not any real hotel's policy.

## Which pets are allowed
Demo Hotel allows up to 2 dogs or cats per room. Combined weight of all pets
in a room may not exceed 50 lbs (about 23 kg). Pets outside this weight limit
are not accepted, including on a per-exception basis. Service animals as
defined by applicable disability law are always welcome and are not subject
to the weight limit or the pet fee below.

## Pet fee
A non-refundable pet fee of $75 per stay, per room applies, regardless of the
number of nights or the number of pets in the room (up to the 2-pet limit).
The fee is charged at check-in and covers additional cleaning; it is separate
from any damage charges described below.

## Where pets are and are not allowed
Pets are welcome in guest rooms and outdoor common areas. Pets are not allowed
in the restaurant, the fitness center, or the pool area at any time. A pet
left unattended in a guest room must be crated and may not be left for more
than 4 hours; housekeeping will not enter a room with an unattended,
uncrated pet.

## Damage and cleanliness
Guests are responsible for any damage caused by a pet, billed at cost after
checkout. A room requiring cleaning beyond the standard pet fee due to odor,
stains, or excessive shedding may be charged an additional cleaning fee of up
to $150.

## Pet-friendly rooms
Pet-friendly rooms are a limited category and should be requested at the time
of booking. Demo Hotel cannot guarantee a pet-friendly room for a reservation
made without a pet noted, and pets are not permitted in rooms not designated
pet-friendly, even temporarily.

## Advance notice
Guests should note pets in a reservation at booking, or at minimum 24 hours
before arrival, so a suitable room can be assigned.
