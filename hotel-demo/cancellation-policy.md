# Cancellation Policy

> Self-authored demo corpus for the A2A demonstration; not any real hotel's policy.

## Standard reservations
A standard (non-prepaid) reservation at Demo Hotel may be cancelled free of
charge up to 48 hours before the scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made
inside that 48-hour window are charged a fee equal to one night's room rate,
posted to the card on file.

## Prepaid and advance-purchase rates
Prepaid rates are charged in full at the time of booking and are non-refundable.
This applies regardless of how far in advance the cancellation request is made.
Guests who anticipate a schedule change should book a standard rate instead of
a prepaid rate.

## No-shows
A reservation not cancelled and not checked into by 11:59 pm on the arrival
date is treated as a no-show. No-shows are charged one night's room rate and
the remaining nights of the reservation are released.

## Group bookings
Reservations of 6 rooms or more booked under one group code follow a separate
30-day cancellation window. Cancelling a group booking fewer than 30 days
before arrival forfeits any deposit paid at booking. Contact the group desk
directly to cancel or modify a group reservation; the standard 48-hour window
does not apply.

## How to cancel
Cancellations can be made online with the confirmation number and the last
name on the reservation, or by phone during front-desk hours. A cancellation
confirmation number is issued for every successful cancellation; keep it as
proof the cancellation was processed before the deadline.

## Exceptions
Demo Hotel may waive cancellation fees at its discretion for documented
emergencies (for example a death in the family or a government-declared
travel restriction affecting the arrival date). Waiver requests are handled
case by case and are not guaranteed.
